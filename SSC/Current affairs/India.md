## Geography
- It shares a border with seven countries, namely [[China]], [[Pakistan]], [[Bhutan]], [[Myanmar]], [[Afghanistan]], [[Nepal]], and [[Bangladesh]]. (PCB BAM)
- India shares the longest border with [[Bangladesh]].
- **It is the largest democracy in the world,** the representative with the most number of votes gets the power.
- [[New Delhi]] is the capital of India.
- [[Droupadi Murmu]] is the current President of India (as of 2026) and [[Narendra Modi]] is the current PM of India as of 2026.
