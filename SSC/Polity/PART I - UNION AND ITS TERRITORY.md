## [[Article 1 - Name and Territory of the Union]]
- [[India]] i.e. [[Bharat]], shall be the [[Union of the States]]
- The states and the territories thereof will be specified in the [[Schedule 1]]
- The territory of India shall be comprise of
    - The territories of the states
    - The union territories specified in the [[Schedule 1]]
    - Such other territories as may be acquired
### Type of Polity
- India - not a Federation, but [[Union of the States]]
- [[Federation of the State]]: 
    - A new state that is formed through a treaty of an agreement between various federating units
- [[Union of the States]] 
    - No agreement between the federating units
    - No right to secede from the Union
- India if Indestructible Union of Destructible States
- Us is Indestructible Union of Indestructible States
## [[Article 2 - Admission or Establishment of new States]]
- [[Parliament (Sansad)]] by law may establish or admit new states on such terms and conditions as it thinks fit
## [[Article 3 - Formation of new States and alternation of areas, boundaries or names of existing states]]
- [[Parliament (Sansad)]] by law
    - Form a state
        - separation of territory from any State
        - Uniting two or more states
        - Parts of states 
        - by uniting any territory to a part of any state
    - Increase area of any state
    - Diminish area of any state
    - Alter the boundaries of any State
    - Alter the name of any State
