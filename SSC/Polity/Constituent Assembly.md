## Timeline
- [[1928]]
    - early draft of [[Indian Constitution]] (Nehru Report) was submitted by [[All Parties Conference]] at [[Lucknow, UP]]
    - chaired by [[Motilal Nehru]]
- [[1934]]
    - [[M.N. Roy]] proposed idea of [[Constituent Assembly]]
- [[1935]]
    - [[Indian National Congress (INC)]] officially demanded [[Constituent Assembly]]
## Offers from Britain
*Reason: Ongoing [[World War II (WW2)]]*
- [[August Offer]] - [[1940]] - rejected
- [[Cripps Proposal]] - [[1942]] - rejected
- [[Wavell Plan]] - [[1945]] - rejected
- [[Cabinet Mission Plan]] - [[1946]] - accepted
### Reasons of rejections of former plans
- [[Indian National Congress (INC)]] demanded [[Purna Swaraj]] 
- Britisher demanded [[Dominion Status]] hence rejected

## Seats Allocation
- [[British Province]] seat allocation were based on three principal communities
    - Muslim
    - Sikh
    - General
- 1 Seat allocation per million population
- partly elected and partly nominated
- Method of election (Indirect Election) - [[Proportional Representation]] + [[Single Transferable Vote]]
- Election held on July-Aug [[1946]]
    - Results - Total seats = 
        - [[Indian National Congress (INC)]] - 208 seats
        - [[Muslim League]] - 73 seats
        - Independent = 15 seats
    - After boycott of [[Muslim League]] (Partition) - 299 remaining

### [[Objective Resolution]]
- Passed by [[Jawahar Lal Nehru]] on [[Dec 13th, 1946]]
- Accepted on [[Jan 22, 1947]] by [[Constituent Assembly]]
- Defined the guiding principles for the Constitution. It established India as an independent sovereign republic, guaranteed fundamental rights to all citizens, and acted as the foundation for the Preamble
