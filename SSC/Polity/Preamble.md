## Understanding the Preamble
### Source of power of Constitution
    - **WE, THE PEOPLE OF INDIA.....**
    - shows that constitution is drawn up and enacted by people through their representatives and not handed down by anyone
### Nature of Constitution
- [[Sovereign]]
    - People have right to take decision on internal as well as external matters
    - No External Power can dictate [[Government of India (GOI)]]
- [[Socialist]]
    - Wealth is generated socially and should be shared equally
    - Government should take care of ownership of land to reduce socio economic inequalities
- [[Secular]]
    - Citizen have complete freedom to choose religion or not choose at all
    - No official state religion
    - All beliefs are treated equally by Government
    - India follow positive concept of secularism (involvement of state in religious matters)
- [[Democratic]]
    - The Government functions according to the constitution and the rules and principles
    - A Government of the People, for the People, by the People
- [[Republic]]
    - Head of state is elected by election by the people
    - not based on hereditary or king hood
### Objectives of the Constitution
- [[Justice]]
    - People cannot be discriminated based on the caste, religion and gender.
    - Social inequalities needs to be reduced
    - Three types - Social, Economical and Political
- [[Liberty]]
    - No unreasonable demands on citizen of India in how the think, express thoughts and actions
    - Five forms of liberty in Preamble
        - Thought
        - Expression
        - Belief 
        - Faith
        - Worship
- [[Equality]]
    - All are equal before law
    - Traditional inequalities must end
    - Government should ensure equal opportunities for all
    - two types - Status and Opportunity
- [[Fraternity]]
    - All of us should behave as if we are part of same family
    - No one is inferior to anyone else

> [!FACT]
> [[Liberty]], [[Fraternity]], [[Equality]] and [[Republic]] - Taken from [[France]]
> Ideals of Justice - Taken from [[Russia]]

## Is Preamble part of the [[Indian Constitution]]
- [[Berubari Union Case, 1960]]
    - Supreme Court:
        - [[Preamble]] not part of the [[Indian Constitution]]
        - Key to understanding of the [[Indian Constitution]]
        - Not legally Enforceable
- [[Kesvananda Bharti Case, 1973]]
    - Supreme Court overruled the [[Berubari Union Case, 1960]]
    - [[Preamble]] part of the [[Indian Constitution]]
    - reflects basic structure of [[Indian Constitution]]
- [[LIC of India Case, 1995]]
    - Reaffirmed the [[Kesvananda Bharti Case, 1973]]

> [!FACT]
> [[Preamble]] amended only once in [[42nd CAA (Amendment) 1976]]
> Added keywords: ISS - [[Integrity]], [[Socialist]], [[Secular]]

## Enforceability of Preamble
- Not Enforceable in Court of Law
- Neither source of power not restriction to the legislature
- Can be considered as aid to the legal interpretation of the constitution
