- Article 326 of the Constitution provides that the elections to the [[House of People (Lok Sabha)|Lok Sabha]] and the [[Legislative Assembly]] of every State shall be based on [[adult suffrage]].
- For that, a person should not be less than 18 years of age.

- The bill of The Constitution [[61st Amendment Act, 1988]] was introduced in the Lok Sabha on 13 December 1988.
- Sixty-first Amendment Act, 1988 reduced the age of a person from 21 to 18 years for vote.
