- Article 368 does not set down the legislative process to be followed at various points of the amendment.

## [[Amendments]]

### [[1st CA (Amendment), 1951]]
- consequential changes from exempting land reforms from scrutiny to providing protections for backward classes in the Constitution.
- zamindari abolition laws in general and certain specified State Acts in particular
- The First Amendment Act amended articles 15, 19, 85, 87, 174, 176, 341, 342, 372, and 376.
- **Added [[Schedule 9]]** to **protect the land reforms** and other laws included in it from judicial review. **After [[Article 31]], [[Articles 31A]] and [[Article 31B]] were inserted.**
